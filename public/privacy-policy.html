Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dough Dealers, LLC ("Dough Dealers," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Dough Dealers mobile app (the "App") to browse our menu and place orders for pickup or delivery.

Information We Collect

When you place an order through the App, we collect the following information directly from you:

  • Name — so we know who the order is for.
  • Email address — to send order confirmations and updates.
  • Phone number — so we can reach you about your order.
  • Delivery address — only if you choose delivery instead of pickup.
  • Order details — the items, quantities, and options you select, along with your order history.

You do not need to create an account or log in to use the App. We only collect the information above when you actively submit an order.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Prepare and fulfill your order (pickup or delivery)
  • Contact you about your order status
  • Provide customer support
  • Keep records of past orders for support and business purposes

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use it for tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes. Your information may be shared only with service providers who help us operate the App and fulfill orders (for example, hosting or app infrastructure providers), and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services on our behalf.

Data Retention

We retain order and contact information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill orders, provide customer support, and comply with our recordkeeping and legal obligations.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

Because the App does not require an account, most of the information we hold about you is tied to the orders you've placed. If you'd like us to delete your information or have questions about what we have on file, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
